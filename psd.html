Free software development

Procyberian Systems Distribution (PSD) is a developer network that supports all open source and free software operating systems. It brings together people who want to produce software, improve operating systems, and make useful technology available to everyone.

PSD is open to all free and open source operating systems and to software developers, contributors, maintainers, and people who are learning to create software.
