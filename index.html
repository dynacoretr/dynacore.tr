DynaCore

DynaCore is a bridge between communities building open, collaborative technology.

Mass Collaboration LabsResearch, development, laboratory and education environment Masscollabs ServicesInternet technologies, telecommunications, datacenters and self-hosted services Procyberian Systems Distribution (PSD)Free and open source operating systems and software development QB NetworksInformation security research community

One ecosystem, many areas of expertise

DynaCore connects QB Networks, Masscollabs Services, Procyberian Systems Distribution, and Mass Collaboration Labs with other community projects. It develops communication protocols and collaboration processes for open networks, including ActivityPub, Matrix, and Mastodon networks.

These structures are built on the four essential freedoms of free software: the freedom to run, study, modify, and share software.

The four freedoms

According to the Free Software Foundation's definition of free software:

  1. Freedom 0: The freedom to run the program for any purpose.
  2. Freedom 1: The freedom to study how the program works and change it to make it do what you wish.
  3. Freedom 2: The freedom to redistribute copies so you can help others.
  4. Freedom 3: The freedom to distribute copies of your modified versions to others.
