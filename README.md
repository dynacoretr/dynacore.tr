# DynaCore

[DynaCore](https://dynacore.tr) is a bridge and coordinating organization that
brings together organizations working with free and open technologies. Our goal
is to help communities with different areas of expertise work together in a
coordinated, transparent, and sustainable way.

## Renewed website

We have renewed our website: [dynacore.tr](https://dynacore.tr)

The website introduces the organizations in the DynaCore ecosystem and their
main areas of work in one place. DynaCore is more than a directory of projects;
it is a coordination center that helps establish shared principles, technical
protocols, and channels for collaboration.

### Website features

The website is a responsive static site with complete English and Turkish page
sets. English pages link to their Turkish `.tr.html` counterparts, and Turkish
pages link back to the corresponding English pages. The localized pages cover
the DynaCore home page and the four core communities: QB Networks, Masscollabs
Services, Procyberian Systems Distribution, and Mass Collaboration Labs.

The home pages describe DynaCore's work on open communication protocols and
collaboration processes, including ActivityPub, Matrix, and Mastodon networks.
They also present the four essential freedoms of free software and link to the
Free Software Foundation's definition.

Every page includes a footer with the GNU AGPLv3-or-later license, a link to
the complete source repository, and a link to the BDFL website. The English
footer uses `Source Code` and the Turkish footer uses `Kaynak Kod`. The
browser-viewable `script.js` file is linked as `JavaScript` on English pages
and `JavaScript Kodu` on Turkish pages. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript source files
include the AGPL copyright and license notice as comments.

## Organizational structure

The four core organizations in the DynaCore ecosystem have different but
complementary responsibilities. Each organization produces work in its own
area, while all four remain connected to DynaCore as part of the wider ecosystem.

### DynaCore

DynaCore is the bridge and coordinating organization for the ecosystem. Its
responsibilities include:

- Developing ethical guidelines and shared principles for collaboration.
- Leading the development of protocols for use in technology.
- Facilitating communication, coordination, and collaboration between organizations.
- Connecting joint projects, standards, and communities.
- Supporting the ecosystem's development as an open, inclusive, free, and
  sustainable community.

Rather than absorbing the other organizations' areas of expertise into a single
center, DynaCore connects those areas and helps align them toward shared goals.

### QB Networks

[QB Networks](https://qbnetworks.xyz) is responsible for information security and infrastructure management.
Its work includes:

- Developing information security practices and security solutions.
- Building, operating, and managing systems, networks, and infrastructure.
- Contributing to reliable, sustainable, and auditable infrastructure.
- Supporting secure and accessible technical resources for the ecosystem.

### Masscollabs Services

[Masscollabs Services](https://masscollabs.xyz) focuses on [self-hosted services](https://source.masscollabs.xyz), internet technologies,
and telecommunications. Its areas of work include:

- Producing self-hosted services that individuals and communities can operate
  under their own control.
- Developing internet services, networking technologies, and telecommunications
  solutions.
- Building open and interoperable systems that support data and service sovereignty.
- Providing deployable, manageable, and sustainable service infrastructure based
  on community needs.

### Procyberian Systems Distribution

[Procyberian Systems Distribution (PSD)](https://procyberian.xyz) works in software development and free
operating systems. Its responsibilities include:

- Writing code and producing software to meet practical needs.
- Supporting free software and open-source operating systems.
- Developing its own free operating system and software products.
- Making it easier for users, developers, and communities to participate in the
  free software ecosystem.

PSD follows a development approach based on software that can be studied,
modified, shared, and reproduced.

### Mass Collaboration Labs

[Mass Collaboration Labs](https://masscollaborationlabs.xyz) is an education, laboratory, and research and
development center. Its primary responsibilities include:

- Organizing technical training, workshops, and learning programs.
- Creating laboratory environments where new ideas and technologies can be tested.
- Conducting research and development projects.
- Strengthening knowledge sharing between organizations and communities.
- Supporting newcomers in gaining experience and contributing to production
  processes.

## Connection and collaboration model

All other organizations in the ecosystem connect with these four core
organizations. The four core organizations are also connected to DynaCore. This
creates a collaboration network in which areas of expertise complement one
another, rather than a one-way hierarchy.

Under this model:

1. DynaCore coordinates the shared ethical framework, communication, and protocol
   development efforts.
2. QB Networks strengthens the security and infrastructure layer.
3. Masscollabs Services develops internet, telecommunications, and self-hosted
   services.
4. Procyberian Systems Distribution carries out coding and free operating system
   work.
5. Mass Collaboration Labs supports education, laboratory work, and research and
   development activities.

This division of responsibilities allows organizations to produce independently
while working together on shared projects.

## Participation and feedback

We welcome your thoughts and suggestions. You can share ideas about the
organizations' areas of work, the protocols, services, software, training, and
research being developed. Criticism, feedback, and new collaboration proposals
help the ecosystem grow.

## Free content principle

All content produced by us is free. Source code, technical documentation,
educational materials, research outputs, and other work are created according
to principles that support sharing, study, modification, and reuse.
