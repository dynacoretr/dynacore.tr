Internet, telecommunications, and datacenters

Masscollabs Services is an organization that provides and supports the development of internet technologies and self-hosted services. It helps communities build open, interoperable, and sustainable services under their own control.

Its work supports the internet, telecommunications, and datacenter sectors. The community is open to system administrators, information technology professionals, and everyone working in IT departments who wants to build and operate community-owned infrastructure.
