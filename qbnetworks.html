Information security research

QB Networks is the information security research community within the DynaCore ecosystem. It supports open source cybersecurity work, security research, infrastructure protection, and the exchange of knowledge.

The community is open to all information security researchers, practitioners, students, and contributors who want to study, improve, and share security knowledge and tools.
